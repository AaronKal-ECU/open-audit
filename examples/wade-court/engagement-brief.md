ENGAGEMENT FRAMING
Four-doctor general practice with three reception and admin staff. The owner's
main concern is that admin staff spend most of the day on static, repetitive
paperwork instead of patient-facing work. The audit shows where that time goes
and which three automations would free the most of it. Clinical judgement stays
with the doctors, and no automation decides anything clinical.

QUESTIONS THE CLIENT ASKED
1. Whether to let patients book telehealth online now. Answer as a next-phase
   item.

WORKFLOW CANDIDATES
- New patient registration: paper form typed into Best Practice, previous
  records requested.
- Results recall: reception phones patients a doctor has flagged for follow-up.
- Care plan review recalls: monthly due report, calls and letters.
- Outgoing referral letters: typed from dictation and sent.
- Medicare rejection rework.

CLIENT FACTS (checked against the transcript)
- New patients: about 20 a week. Form typing 10 minutes (0.167 hrs) each,
  records request 5 minutes (0.083 hrs) each.
- Results recall: about 30 calls a week at 5 minutes each; about a third need a
  second call.
- Care plan recalls: about 4 hours a month.
- Referral letters: about 40 a week at 6 minutes (0.1 hrs) each.
- Medicare rejections: about 10 a week at 10 minutes each.
- Existing tools: Best Practice, HotDoc, Outlook.
